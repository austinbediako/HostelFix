# Sources

## University of Ghana Institutional Scope

- [University of Ghana Accommodation](https://www.ug.edu.gh/aad/accomodation) - identifies the five traditional halls and four UGEL halls, and states that external accommodation services are not recognised or endorsed.
- [University of Ghana Leadership](https://ug.edu.gh/about-ug/leadership) - identifies Hall Heads and relevant administrative directorates, including physical/development services and IT.
- [University of Ghana Housing Regulations](https://www.ug.edu.gh/pad/sites/pad/files/2025-03/Revised%20UG%20Housing%20Regulations-Final%2020th%20July%202024.pdf) - describes formal maintenance requests and the role of PDMSD for University-provided accommodation.
